# Winner Selection

**Winner:** Coca Cola 300ml plastic bottle front

**URL:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7f/Coca_cola_bottle_-_300ml_plastic_-_front_-_01.jpg

**Criteria met:**
- **Look:** Classic, instantly recognizable bottle shape and color scheme.
- **Brand clarity:** The Coca‑Cola logo is large, centered, and legible.
- **Everyday appeal:** This image represents the everyday soda experience most consumers see.

**Why the other images were not chosen:**
- **Coca Cola Cherry Vanilla Life:** While it shows a unique flavor, the image is less iconic and the label is smaller, making it less immediately recognizable.
- **Coca Cola soda in store:** The setting is generic and the bottle is partially obscured by other products, reducing brand visibility.
